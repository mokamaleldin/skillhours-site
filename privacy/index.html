PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy, kept simple.

Effective date: November 15, 2026

SkillHours is designed to work offline and does not require an account for its core functionality.

Information stored by SkillHours

SkillHours stores information that you create in the app, such as skills, hour targets, practice sessions, session notes, timer state, milestones, and app preferences. This information is stored locally on your device.

Data collection

The current release of SkillHours does not operate a developer-controlled account system, cloud sync service, advertising system, or analytics service. SkillHours does not intentionally transmit your learning data to developer-operated servers.

Tracking and advertising

SkillHours does not use your activity for cross-app tracking and does not include advertising in the current release.

Third-party platforms

Your device operating system, the App Store, and distribution or update infrastructure may process technical information according to their own privacy policies. Those services are operated independently of SkillHours.

Data retention and deletion

Because SkillHours stores app data locally, it remains on your device until you delete it through the app, reset the app's data, or remove the application and its stored data. SkillHours does not maintain a remote copy that the developer can restore for you.

Children's privacy

SkillHours does not knowingly collect personal information from children through developer-operated servers.

Changes to this policy

If SkillHours changes how it handles data in a future release, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect those changes.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit the SkillHours Support page or contact the developer through GitHub.
