SUPPORT

How can we help?

If you are having a problem with SkillHours, have feedback, or want to report a bug, you can contact the developer through GitHub.

SkillHours Support GitHub: @mokamaleldin
Support requests: Open a GitHub issue

Before reporting an issue

  • Include your iPhone model and iOS version when relevant.
  • Describe what you expected to happen and what actually happened.
  • Do not include passwords or sensitive personal information.

Your data

SkillHours is designed as an offline-first app. Your skills, practice sessions, timer state, and preferences are stored locally on your device. If you remove the app or erase its local data, that information may no longer be recoverable.

Privacy

For details about how SkillHours handles data, read the Privacy Policy.
